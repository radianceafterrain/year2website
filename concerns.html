Awareness of Legal and Ethical concerns


Audience


Our game Revanent Roulette is directed towards all ages but particularly children in class who want to pass the time

This means that during all stages of development we'll need to keep in mind that what content and gameplay we're presenting to our audience is appropriate and healthy

Notably, the word 'Roulette' being in the title may gain association with things like micro-transactions and ingame loot crates which can be very harmful for even adults, so we need to be especially careful when dealing with a younger impressionable audience

(Though there won't be any sort of transaction and the game'll be free so it should be straight forward to not encourage any unhealthy gambling addictions)
